

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an **interactive Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze business data and present meaningful insights through visualizations, KPIs, filters, and interactive reports.

The dashboard helps users understand **sales performance, profit, orders, quantity, product categories, regions, and customer segments** in an easy-to-understand visual format.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall business performance
* Track important KPIs such as Sales, Profit, Orders, and Quantity
* Identify profitable and low-performing categories
* Compare regional performance
* Analyze customer segments
* Provide interactive filtering and drill-down capabilities
* Support data-driven business decision-making

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* **Power BI**
* **Power Query**
* **DAX**
* **Data Visualization**
* **Microsoft Excel / CSV Dataset**

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🔹 KPI Cards

The dashboard displays important business metrics such as:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Total Quantity
* Profit Margin

### 🔹 Interactive Visualizations

* Sales by Category
* Profit by Category
* Regional Sales Analysis
* Customer Segment Analysis
* Sales Trend Analysis
* Product Performance
* Profitability Analysis

### 🔹 Filters & Slicers

Users can dynamically filter the dashboard based on:

* Region
* Category
* Segment
* Ship Mode
* Order Date

5. Use the available slicers and visualizations to explore the dashboard.
